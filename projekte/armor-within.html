Armor Within

"Armor Within" explores fashion as modern armor, combining structured silhouettes with soft, flowing fabrics.

Inspired by historical protective wear and hunting aesthetics, the collection contrasts rigid materials such as leather and denim with delicate lace to balance strength and elegance.

The project translates protection, structure, and modern femininity into a contemporary wearable fashion language.
